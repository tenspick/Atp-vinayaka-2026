WELCOME TO

ANANTHAMPALLI

Where devotion becomes a celebration.

Ananthampalli Village comes together to celebrate Vinayaka Chavithi with devotion, tradition, culture, and community spirit. Join us for grand daily poojas, cultural dramas, sacred Annadanam, and the grand procession.

WHAT'S HAPPENING

FESTIVAL PROGRAMS

SEP 14

GANAPATHI POOJA & INSTALLATION

Vedic rituals, holy chanting, and official installation of Lord Vinayaka idol at the main mandap.

⏰ 06:00 AM - 08:00 AM 📍 Main Mandap
SEP 15

BHAKTI SANGEET & CULTURAL DRAMA

Traditional songs, dance performances by village youth, and devotional music concert.

⏰ 06:00 PM - 09:00 PM 📍 Village Stage
SEP 23

GRAND NIMAJJANAM PROCESSION

Procession with Dhol Tasha, colors, music, and eco-friendly immersion ceremony.

⏰ 02:00 PM - 09:00 PM 📍 Ananthampalli
