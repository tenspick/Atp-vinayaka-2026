ABOUT ANANTHAMPALLI FESTIVAL

Celebrating Faith, Harmony, Culture & Eco-Friendly Devotion in Ananthampalli Village

🏛️ Ananthampalli Village Vinayaka Chavithi Legacy

Vinayaka Chavithi in Ananthampalli Village is a grand annual festival where all families, youth, elders, and devotees gather under one roof to celebrate Lord Ganesha with immense devotion, joy, and togetherness.

From daily morning Vedic poojas to evening Bhakti Sangeet, cultural dramas performed by village children, sacred Annadanam food offerings, and the final grand Visarjan procession, our festival embodies true community unity.

🌱 100% Eco-Friendly Clay Ganesha Commitment

In Ananthampalli, we are committed to protecting Mother Nature. Our village idol is crafted purely from organic clay and natural bio-degradable colors without any harmful Plaster of Paris (PoP) or toxic chemicals.

Our Nimajjanam ceremony respects our village water bodies, preserving water purity for future generations.
